**Abstract:** Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown remarkable performance across various tasks, but the lack of comprehensive evaluation benchmarks hinders understanding of their true capabilities. In this paper, we introduce C-Eval, a multi-level multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite designed to comprehensively assess foundation models. C-Eval covers multiple disciplines and difficulty levels, providing a standardized framework for evaluating Chinese LLMs. Our extensive experiments reveal significant insights into the strengths and weaknesses of current foundation models in Chinese language understanding and reasoning.
