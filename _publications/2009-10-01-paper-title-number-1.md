**Abstract:** Logical reasoning is a fundamental aspect of artificial intelligence, yet current approaches struggle with synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale. In this paper, we introduce SynLogic, a novel framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data that enables models to learn logical reasoning more effectively. Our method generates large-scale, high-quality reasoning datasets with built-in verifiability, allowing for more robust training and evaluation of reasoning capabilities. Extensive experiments demonstrate significant improvements across multiple logical reasoning benchmarks.
